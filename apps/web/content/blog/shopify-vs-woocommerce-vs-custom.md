### Start with your real business needs

Every platform has strengths, but the right decision depends on what your business actually needs now and in the next 12 to 24 months, not just what looks best in a demo.

### Shopify

Shopify is ideal when you want speed, simplicity, and an easier path to launch. It works especially well for brands with standard catalogs and a need to move quickly, since hosting, security, and checkout are already handled for you.

### WooCommerce

WooCommerce is more flexible and gives you more control over your WordPress environment. It is a solid choice when your store needs deeper customisation, a content-heavy storefront, or tighter integration with existing WordPress content and SEO work.

### Custom builds

Custom builds are best when your catalog, workflow, products, or business logic are too unique for a template to handle comfortably. They take more time and budget upfront, but remove the constraints that platform-based stores eventually run into.

### The right choice is rarely the most popular one

The right platform is the one that fits your growth model, inventory complexity, and team capabilities without creating unnecessary technical friction. Popularity is a poor substitute for fit.

### When in doubt, plan for where you're headed, not just where you are

A platform that suits your store today may not suit it at double the order volume. Choosing with your next stage of growth in mind avoids a costly rebuild down the line.
