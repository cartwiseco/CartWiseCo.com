### Why the choice of partner matters more than the platform

Two stores built on the same platform can perform completely differently depending on who builds them. The technology is rarely the deciding factor — the team behind it is.

### Look at their process, not just their portfolio

A polished portfolio tells you what a company can design. It does not tell you how they handle scope changes, timelines, or post-launch support. Ask how a project actually runs from kickoff to launch.

### Check platform depth, not just platform breadth

A company that claims to build on every platform may not be deeply skilled in any of them. Ask specifically about their experience with the platform your business needs — Shopify, WooCommerce, or a custom build.

### Ask what happens after launch

Launch day is the start of a store's life, not the end of the project. Clarify who handles bug fixes, updates, and scaling support once the site is live, and what that costs.

### Make sure they ask about your business, not just your design preferences

A good development partner asks about your catalog size, order volume, shipping complexity, and growth plans before recommending a platform. If a company skips straight to design without this, that's a signal.

### The right partner fits your stage, not just your budget

The cheapest option and the most expensive option can both be wrong. The right choice is the team that understands where your business is now and where it's headed in the next 12 to 24 months.
