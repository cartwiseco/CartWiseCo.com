### Payment options directly affect conversion rates

Customers abandon checkout when their preferred payment method isn't available. Offering the right mix of options for your audience matters as much as the design of the checkout page itself.

### UPI is not optional for the Indian market

For most Indian e-commerce stores, UPI has become a default expectation rather than a nice-to-have. A checkout without it puts up unnecessary friction for a large share of customers.

### Compare settlement time and fees, not just the brand name

Payment gateways differ in settlement speed, transaction fees, and dispute handling. These operational details affect your cash flow more than most business owners expect going in.

### Match the gateway to your platform

Shopify, WooCommerce, and custom builds each support different gateways with different levels of native integration. Choosing a gateway your platform supports well avoids extra development work and maintenance.

### Security and compliance aren't optional details

PCI compliance and secure checkout are baseline requirements, not extras. A properly integrated gateway handles this for you, but it still needs to be verified during testing, not assumed.

### Test with real transactions before launch

Sandbox testing catches obvious errors, but running a small set of real transactions before full launch surfaces issues that testing environments often miss.
