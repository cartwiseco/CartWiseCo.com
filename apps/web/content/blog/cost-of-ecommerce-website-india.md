### There is no single number that fits every store

E-commerce pricing depends on the platform, the number of products, the integrations needed, and how much customisation the design requires. Anyone quoting a fixed price without asking about your business is guessing.

### Platform choice sets the starting range

A Shopify store with standard features typically costs less to build than a heavily customised WooCommerce site or a fully custom build, because the underlying platform already handles core functionality.

### Integrations add cost in ways that are easy to underestimate

Payment gateways, shipping partners, inventory sync, and marketing tools each take setup time. A store that looks simple on the surface can still involve real integration work behind the scenes.

### Design complexity is a bigger factor than most people expect

A store built on a template with light customisation costs far less than a fully custom design system. Decide early how much of your brand identity needs to be reflected in unique layouts versus proven, conversion-tested patterns.

### Don't forget the ongoing costs

Hosting, domain renewal, app subscriptions, and maintenance continue after launch. A realistic budget accounts for the first year of running the store, not just the build.

### Get a quote based on your actual catalog and goals

The most accurate number comes from a conversation about your products, expected order volume, and growth plans — not a generic price list.
